# Stakeholder Analysis – IT Service Request Management

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the proposed stakeholders for the IT Service Request and Help Desk Workflow Monitoring and Reporting solution.

It outlines their roles, responsibilities, interests, information needs and suggested communication approaches.

**Note:** Stakeholders are proposed personas for this portfolio case study. No actual stakeholder interviews have been conducted.

## 2. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder ID | Stakeholder Role | Key Responsibilities | Interest in the Solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| STK-001 | IT Operations Manager | Monitor overall IT service performance and operational trends | High |
| STK-002 | IT Service Desk Manager | Oversee ticket handling, prioritisation and resolution processes | High |
| STK-003 | Help Desk Team Lead | Monitor team workload, ticket assignments and workflow progress | High |
| STK-004 | IT Support Analyst | Investigate, update and resolve assigned tickets | Medium |

## 3. Stakeholder Needs and Expectations

### STK-001: IT Operations Manager

**Business needs:**
- Understand overall ticket volume and historical trends.
- Monitor operational performance.
- Identify recurring service issues.
- Access consolidated management reports.

**Expected benefit:** Better visibility into help desk operations and historical performance patterns.

### STK-002: IT Service Desk Manager

**Business needs:**
- Monitor ticket status and priority distribution.
- Review resolution duration.
- Identify tickets requiring additional attention.
- Understand workflow bottlenecks.

**Expected benefit:** Improved visibility into ticket handling and potential process improvement areas.

### STK-003: Help Desk Team Lead

**Business needs:**
- Monitor ticket assignments.
- Review workload distribution.
- Identify tickets with prolonged processing.
- Track workflow transitions.

**Expected benefit:** Better understanding of team workload and ticket progression.

### STK-004: IT Support Analyst

**Business needs:**
- View assigned ticket information.
- Understand ticket status and priority.
- Review ticket history and workflow updates.

**Expected benefit:** Easier access to relevant ticket information.

## 4. Stakeholder Interest and Influence Matrix

| Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT Operations Manager | High | High | Periodic summary reports and key findings |
| IT Service Desk Manager | High | High | Requirement reviews and reporting demonstrations |
| Help Desk Team Lead | Medium | High | Workflow reviews and feedback sessions |
| IT Support Analyst | Low | Medium | Usability feedback and report review |

*Influence and interest levels are proposed assumptions for this case study.*

## 5. Communication Plan

| Stakeholder | Information to Communicate | Suggested Method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT Operations Manager | Historical trends and consolidated reports | Management summary | Monthly |
| IT Service Desk Manager | Ticket status, priorities and resolution analysis | Reporting review | Weekly |
| Help Desk Team Lead | Assignment activity and workflow analysis | Team review | Weekly |
| IT Support Analyst | Ticket details and status information | Reporting interface | As needed |

## 6. Business Analyst Responsibilities

For this proposed project, the Business Analyst would:

1. Identify relevant stakeholder groups.
2. Understand stakeholder expectations and reporting needs.
3. Gather and document business requirements.
4. Clarify conflicting or incomplete requirements.
5. Maintain traceability between business needs and functional requirements.
6. Coordinate requirement reviews and UAT feedback.
7. Document agreed changes and follow-up actions.

## 7. Assumptions and Limitations

- Stakeholder roles are proposed personas based on a typical IT help desk environment.
- Influence, interest and communication frequency are illustrative assumptions.
- No actual stakeholder interviews or requirement approval sessions have been conducted.
- The proposed reporting solution is a portfolio concept and has not been implemented in a production environment.
