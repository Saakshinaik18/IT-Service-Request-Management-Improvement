# Stakeholder Register

## Project Name
IT Service Request Management Improvement

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify individuals and teams involved in or affected by the IT service request management process.

It helps the Business Analyst understand stakeholder responsibilities, expectations and information requirements.

## 2. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder | Role | Responsibilities | Expectations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employees | Requesters | Submit IT service requests and provide issue details | Easy request submission and status tracking |
| IT Support Team | Service Providers | Review, assign and resolve requests | Clear priorities and request information |
| IT Team Lead | Team Supervisor | Monitor workload and manage escalations | Team workload visibility |
| IT Service Manager | Service Owner | Monitor service performance and SLA compliance | Performance reports and escalation visibility |
| System Administrator | Technical Support | Manage system configuration and access | Secure and reliable system functionality |
| Business Analyst | Project Analyst | Gather, analyse and document business requirements | Clear stakeholder requirements and agreement |

## 3. Stakeholder Needs

### Employees
- Submit service requests easily.
- Receive request confirmation.
- Track request status.
- Receive updates on resolution progress.

### IT Support Team
- View assigned requests.
- Identify request priority.
- Update ticket status.
- Record resolution details.

### IT Team Lead
- Monitor open and pending requests.
- Identify overdue requests.
- Review team workload.
- Manage escalated tickets.

### IT Service Manager
- Monitor service performance.
- Review SLA compliance.
- Identify recurring operational issues.
- Access management reports.

### System Administrator
- Manage user access.
- Maintain system configurations.
- Support system availability and security.

### Business Analyst
- Understand business requirements.
- Identify process gaps.
- Document functional requirements.
- Validate proposed workflows with stakeholders.

## 4. Stakeholder Communication Plan

| Stakeholder | Communication Method | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Employees | Interviews / Surveys | Understand request submission challenges |
| IT Support Team | Workshops | Understand current ticket handling |
| IT Team Lead | Meetings | Understand assignment and escalation processes |
| IT Service Manager | Review Meetings | Understand reporting and SLA requirements |
| System Administrator | Technical Discussions | Understand system constraints |

## 5. Assumptions

- The stakeholder roles are defined for a fictional organization.
- Stakeholder expectations are proposed assumptions for this case study.
- Actual stakeholder interviews have not been conducted.
- Requirements will be refined as the project analysis progresses.

## 6. Outcome

The stakeholder register provides an initial understanding of project participants, their responsibilities, expectations and communication requirements.
