# Business Problem Statement

## IT Service Request and Help Desk Workflow Improvement

**Project Type:** Independent Business Analyst Portfolio Case Study

**Industry Context:** IT Service Management

**Project Status:** Historical Data Analysis and Proposed Process Improvement

**Document Version:** 2.0

## 1. Project Overview

This project uses an anonymized historical IT help desk dataset covering January 2016 to March 2023 to understand ticket handling patterns, workflow activity and operational reporting requirements.

The objective is to analyse the available ticket information, identify potential process improvement opportunities and define requirements for a proposed Help Desk Operations Monitoring and Reporting solution.

This is an independent portfolio case study and does not represent work performed for a specific employer.

## 2. Business Background

IT help desk environments handle different types of technical issues and service requests. Historical ticket records can provide information about:

- Ticket volume and issue categories.
- Ticket priorities and statuses.
- Ticket creation and resolution timestamps.
- Assignment and contributor activity.
- Workflow stages and transitions.
- Historical processing and resolution duration.

Organising and analysing this information can help operations teams understand workload patterns and identify areas that may require further investigation.

## 3. Problem Statement

Historical help desk ticket information contains multiple dimensions, including issue type, priority, status, assignment activity and workflow history.

Without consolidated reporting and clearly defined metrics, it may be difficult for operational users to understand ticket distribution, monitor workflow progression, review resolution duration and identify potential processing bottlenecks.

This case study therefore explores how historical ticket data can support operational visibility and how business requirements can be translated into a proposed reporting solution.

The identified opportunities are analytical hypotheses and should not be treated as confirmed organisational problems.

## 4. Business Objectives

1. Understand the structure and available fields in the historical ticket dataset.
2. Analyse ticket volume, priority and status patterns.
3. Examine ticket resolution duration where valid timestamps are available.
4. Review assignment activity and contributor involvement.
5. Analyse workflow stages and repeated transitions.
6. Identify potential areas for improved operational reporting.
7. Document business and functional requirements.
8. Propose an improved workflow and reporting approach.

## 5. Project Scope

### In Scope

- Historical ticket data review.
- Ticket volume and category analysis.
- Priority and status analysis.
- Resolution duration analysis using valid timestamps.
- Assignment and contributor analysis.
- Workflow transition analysis.
- Business problem definition.
- Stakeholder persona analysis.

- Gap analysis.

## 7. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a Help Desk Operations Monitoring and Reporting concept.

It aims to provide a consolidated view of:

- Ticket volume and issue categories.
- Priority distribution.
- Ticket status.
- Resolution duration.
- Assignment activity.
- Workflow stage duration and transitions.
- Historical ticket trends.

The solution is a proposed reporting concept, not a newly developed or deployed ticketing system.

## 8. Expected Business Benefits

The proposed reporting approach is expected to support:

- Better visibility into historical ticket patterns.
- Easier review of priority and status distribution.
- Improved understanding of ticket processing duration.
- Better visibility into assignment and workflow activity.
- More structured operational reporting.
- Data-supported identification of potential improvement opportunities.

These benefits are expected outcomes and have not been measured through implementation.

## 9. Assumptions and Constraints

### Assumptions

- The dataset contains anonymized historical help desk ticket information.
- Available fields can support selected ticket, assignment and workflow analyses.
- Proposed stakeholders represent typical roles in an IT help desk environment.
- Proposed process improvements require validation before real-world adoption.

### Constraints

- The case study uses historical data rather than a live ticketing system.
- The As-Is process is inferred from available dataset fields and is not a verified organisational SOP.
- No actual stakeholder interviews have been conducted.
- The To-Be workflow is a recommendation, not an implemented process.
- SLA compliance cannot be assessed without approved SLA targets and business calendar rules.
- UAT test cases are planned scenarios and have not been executed against a live application.

## 11. Conclusion

This case study demonstrates how a Business Analyst can use historical IT help desk data to understand operational information needs, define business requirements, analyse workflows and propose reporting improvements.

It brings together key Business Analysis practices, from problem definition and stakeholder analysis to requirements documentation, process modelling, UAT planning and traceability.
